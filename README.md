# Engineering-Firm-Revenue-Performance-Metrics
Comprehensive Dashboard Analysis and Business Insights

# Dashboard Overview
•	The dashboard evaluates revenue, profitability, discounts, losses, and sales performance across products and countries.

•	The analysis identifies high-performing products, operational inefficiencies, and strategic growth opportunities.

•	Markets analyzed include Canada, France, Germany, Mexico, and the United States.

# Executive Summary of the key performance Metrics

•	Total Profit: $71,044,067.615

•	Total Loss: $58,980,230.36

•	Total Units Sold: $1,125,806.00

•	Total Discounts: $9,205,248.24

•	Total Sales: $118,726,350.20

# Product Sales Performance

•	AeroPeak generated the highest sales revenue at $33,011,143.950.

•	EchoStick and LuminaCore also contributed strongly to overall revenue.

•	VelvetDrift recorded the lowest sales performance among all products.

•	Revenue performance is heavily driven by AeroPeak.

# Profitability Insights

•	AeroPeak generated the highest profit at $29,818,681.60000001.

•	FrostBrew and VelvetDrift delivered strong profitability with zero recorded losses.

•	LuminaCore generated only $224,847.2 profit despite strong sales revenue.

•	Some products generate high revenue but weak profit margins.

# Loss and Discount Analysis

•	LuminaCore recorded the highest losses at $29,019,655.392000005.

•	EchoStick generated losses of $17,455,856.16.

•	AeroPeak received the highest discounts totaling $2,600,518.05.

•	Heavy discounting may be reducing overall profitability.

# Country-Level Performance

•	Canada recorded the strongest AeroPeak sales performance.

•	France and Mexico showed strong EchoStick demand.

•	The United States and Germany demonstrated stable NovaGloss sales.

•	Market performance varies significantly across countries.
 
 # SWOT Analysis

•	Strengths: Strong sales performance and high customer demand.

•	Weaknesses: High operational losses and discount dependency.

•	Opportunities: Expand profitable products and optimize pricing strategies.

•	Threats: Rising costs and overdependence on key products.

# Recommendations

•	Conduct cost optimization for LuminaCore and EchoStick.

•	Reduce excessive discounting to improve profit margins.

•	Scale FrostBrew and VelvetDrift due to strong profitability performance.

•	Focus future strategy on profitability and operational efficiency.

# Conclusion

•	The company demonstrates strong sales and market demand driven primarily by AeroPeak.

Profitability is being affected by substantial operational losses from specific products.

•	Improving cost efficiency and margin growth will strengthen long-term sustainability.
